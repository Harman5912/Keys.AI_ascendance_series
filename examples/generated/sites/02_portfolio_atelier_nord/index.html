About Atelier Nord

Atelier Nord is a small architecture studio based in Oslo, Norway. We specialize in creating unique and sustainable designs for both residential and commercial projects.

To create spaces that are not only functional but also aesthetically pleasing and environmentally friendly.

Our team consists of experienced architects, designers, and engineers who work together to bring your vision to life.

Atelier Nord is committed to sustainability, innovation, and excellence in all our projects.
