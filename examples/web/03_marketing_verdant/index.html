Two hectares, one drying shed

The herbs are grown on a two-hectare strip above the Levels. Everything is cut by hand between June and September and dried in the shed behind the barn — the same building the oils are blended in.

If a harvest is short, we sell out. We do not buy the missing ingredient from a wholesaler and pretend the batch is the same.
