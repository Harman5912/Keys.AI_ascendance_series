About Verdant

Verdant is a small-batch organic skincare brand in Somerset, known for its nourishing and effective products.

Founded by Sarah and Tom, Verdant has been making natural skincare since 2015.

To provide high-quality organic skincare that is gentle on the skin and good for the planet.

We believe in transparency, sustainability, and ethical practices in all aspects of our business.
