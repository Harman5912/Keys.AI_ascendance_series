Reasonable questions

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay a percentage of successful payments only. Failed attempts, refunds and voids cost nothing.

How long does onboarding take?

A UK limited company can be live in about ten minutes. Sole traders usually clear verification the same day.

What about chargebacks?

We handle the evidence pack and the deadline. If a dispute is lost on a transaction we processed correctly, the fee is capped at £15.
